# Music credits

Tunes play softly under the weather sound in serene moments (rain, sunrise, sunset,
late night) — see `utils/ambience.js`.

Processing: loudness-matched to the weather sounds (-20 LUFS), 50 ms fade-in (no click),
1.2 s fade-out baked in, AAC 96 kbps stereo. Original kept in `raw-audio/` (not in git).
